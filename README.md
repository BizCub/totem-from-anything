## Totem from Anything

This mod makes the Totem of Undying activate from any source of damage. In vanilla Minecraft some damage types bypass the totem such as the void or the kill command. With this mod the totem will save you in every situation.

![preview](https://i.postimg.cc/BZ7xT8Kk/1.gif)

## Usage

Just hold a Totem of Undying in your main hand or offhand. Whenever you take fatal damage the totem will activate and save your life no matter what kind of damage it was.

![preview](https://i.postimg.cc/dtzrfznp/2.gif)

## Features

* Works with any damage source even those that normally ignore totems
* No configuration needed just install and play
* Lightweight changes only the totem check
* Available for multiple mod loaders
